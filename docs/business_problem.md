# Business Problem

## Project

Enterprise Knowledge Copilot

## Problem

Organisations often store important knowledge across large collections of
internal documents, including HR policies, security procedures, compliance
documents, operational manuals, and technical documentation.

Employees may spend significant time searching these documents to find the
information required to complete their work or make decisions.

This can lead to:

- inefficient information retrieval;
- repeated questions to specialist teams;
- use of outdated information;
- inconsistent answers;
- difficulty locating the source of information.

## Proposed Solution

The Enterprise Knowledge Copilot will provide employees with a conversational
interface for querying approved organisational knowledge.

Rather than relying only on an LLM's existing knowledge, the system will
eventually retrieve relevant information from approved internal documents and
use that information to generate source-grounded responses.

## Project Objective

The objective is to design, build, evaluate, and deploy an enterprise-oriented
Generative AI application that provides authorised users with reliable and
traceable answers from organisational knowledge.

The project will be developed incrementally, with each architectural component
introduced only when its purpose and behaviour are understood.
